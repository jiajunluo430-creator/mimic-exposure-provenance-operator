# Post-primary exploratory addendum

Date locked: 2026-07-29, after completion and validation of the frozen
primary analysis.

## Purpose

This addendum permits descriptive analyses needed to prepare a methods-focused
submission. It does not amend the frozen contract, medication whitelist,
event semantics, analysis windows, anchor cohorts, covariates, outcomes, or
effect-change thresholds.

## Complete analysis set

The exploratory layer must report all of the following, irrespective of
statistical significance or direction:

1. All six frozen medication classes:
   - order nonconversion percentage;
   - documented administrations per eligible order;
   - negative-lag and greater-than-24-hour lag percentages;
   - primary and audit-semantic not-given percentages;
   - absolute and relative semantic-sensitivity change.
2. Both frozen published anchors:
   - the full 2 x 2 cross-classification of order-defined and
     administration-defined exposure;
   - observed agreement, Cohen kappa, Jaccard agreement, order-only and
     administration-only fractions;
   - descriptive outcome frequency in every observed discordance cell.
3. Every already fitted anchor model variant:
   - order and administration estimates;
   - movement toward or away from the null on the log-effect scale;
   - the frozen material-change classification.

## Interpretation boundary

- The exploratory layer is descriptive and hypothesis-generating.
- Documented eMAR administration is not called a clinical gold standard.
- Associations remain exposure-definition sensitivity analyses, not new
  causal efficacy or safety estimates.
- No medication class, anchor, subgroup, covariate, window, or model may be
  selected or suppressed because of a P value or effect direction.
- eICU remains an interface-semantic contrast and is not external validation.
