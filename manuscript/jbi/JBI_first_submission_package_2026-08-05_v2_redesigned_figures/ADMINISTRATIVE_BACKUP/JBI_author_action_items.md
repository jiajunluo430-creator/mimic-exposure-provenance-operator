# Author action items before submission

These are administrative confirmations, not analysis blockers.

- Confirm all five authors have reviewed and approved the final JBI version.
- Confirm author order, equal-contribution notes, affiliations, and corresponding-author roles.
- Confirm the National Natural Science Foundation and Chongqing grant wording.
- Confirm that no local institutional review approval was separately required or obtained for this credentialed secondary analysis; do not invent a local approval number.
- Add a telephone number only if the submission portal makes it mandatory.
- Choose the no-cost subscription/green route unless the author team affirmatively selects and funds gold open access.
- Upload the separate title page only if the portal supplies that designation; JBI’s stated review model is single anonymized, and the main manuscript already contains title-page information.
- Do not upload restricted MIMIC-IV/eICU data, row-level outputs, or local path manifests.
- Do not claim independent second coding or Cohen kappa unless a completed second-coder worksheet is genuinely produced.
- Do not push, create a public release, deposit a preprint, or submit the manuscript without corresponding-author authorization.
